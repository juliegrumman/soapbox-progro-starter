## Executive Summary

1. **ProGRO Density+ is invisible on Google.** Across 28 high-value keywords totaling ~420K monthly searches, the ProGRO product page does not appear in the top 10 for a single one. The only soapboxsoaps.com ranking is for "soapbox hair growth serum" (#1) — but it serves the "Oh My Growth" product, not ProGRO.
2. **Rosemary oil hair growth is the highest-volume opportunity** at ~74K monthly searches with purely informational/editorial results — no DTC brand ranks. A well-crafted educational content piece could capture significant traffic.
3. **Vegamour and The Ordinary dominate the category SERP landscape.** Vegamour holds position #1 for "hair growth serum" (33K/mo); The Ordinary holds #1 for both "hair density serum" and "peptide hair treatment." These are the primary SEO competitors.
4. **The "postpartum hair loss" cluster is high-volume (12K+/mo), rising, and wide open** — no DTC brand ranks in the top 10. Given the competitive intel showing postpartum recovery as a high-value persona, this is a prime content opportunity.
5. **Branded search is near-zero.** "ProGRO density plus serum" has ~210 monthly searches; "soapbox hair growth serum" has ~390. Category search drives 99.8% of discoverable demand — ProGRO must win on category and problem keywords, not brand.

## Cluster Analysis

### Hair Growth (4 keywords | 138,500 monthly volume)
- **Who dominates:** Vegamour (#1 for "hair growth serum"), The Ordinary (#1 for "hair density serum"), Amazon and retailers for generic terms
- **ProGRO presence:** Absent from all results
- **Trends:** "Hair serum" and "hair growth serum" both rising. "Scalp serum" also rising.
- **Opportunity:** These are the highest-volume category terms. ProGRO needs to appear in editorial roundups (Allure, Elle, Shape) and retailer searches (Ulta, Sephora, Amazon) to compete. Product page optimization and PR/seeding to beauty editors are the paths in.

### Thinning/Loss (6 keywords | 78,600 monthly volume)
- **Who dominates:** Medical/authority sites (Mayo Clinic, Cleveland Clinic, WebMD) for informational terms; Sephora and Ulta for commercial terms. Vegamour appears at #7 for "best product for thinning hair."
- **ProGRO presence:** Absent
- **Trends:** "Thinning hair treatment" and "hair loss serum" both rising significantly.
- **Opportunity:** Medical sites are hard to displace, but the commercial terms ("hair loss serum," "best product for thinning hair," "hair thickening serum") are winnable through retailer presence and review-driven authority. "Postpartum hair loss treatment" is a high-opportunity gap — no DTC brand ranks, and this is ProGRO's strongest persona from the competitive intel.

### Ingredients (4 keywords | 87,800 monthly volume)
- **Who dominates:** "Rosemary oil hair growth" (74K) is owned by Healthline and Cleveland Clinic. "Peptide hair treatment" and "hair density serum" are owned by The Ordinary. "Biotin hair serum" and "redensyl hair serum" have no strong incumbent.
- **ProGRO presence:** Absent
- **Trends:** Rosemary oil peaked in March 2026 — massive and growing. Peptide hair treatment rising from a small base.
- **Opportunity:** Rosemary oil content is the single biggest traffic opportunity. A blog post like "Rosemary Oil vs. Peptide Serums for Hair Growth: What the Science Says" could capture traffic from the 74K/mo rosemary oil audience while positioning ProGRO's peptide formulation as the advanced alternative.

### Comparison (5 keywords | 66,400 monthly volume)
- **Who dominates:** Editorial roundups (Ulta, Glamour, The Quality Edit) and third-party comparison sites. Neither Divi nor Vegamour own their own comparison SERPs.
- **ProGRO presence:** Absent
- **Trends:** "Best hair growth serum" strongly rising (interest nearly doubled in 12 months). "Divi scalp serum" stable.
- **Opportunity:** Getting featured in "best of" roundups is the primary strategy. Also: ProGRO could create comparison content ("ProGRO vs. Divi vs. Vegamour") to insert itself into the consideration set. Third-party review sites are open to new brand entries.

### Education (6 keywords | 29,800 monthly volume)
- **Who dominates:** Reddit, YouTube, editorial (Today, InStyle). UGC and social content dominate "before and after" and "how long for results."
- **ProGRO presence:** Absent
- **Trends:** "Does hair growth serum work" and "hair growth serum before and after" are growing — purchase-intent research is increasing.
- **Opportunity:** This is where content marketing wins. Every educational query maps to a specific content piece (see Content Opportunities below). These searchers are in the research phase — capture them early and convert later.

### Scalp Care (1 keyword | 18,100 monthly volume)
- **Who dominates:** Editorial roundups (Elle, The Quality Edit). Competitors appear in shopping results but not organic.
- **ProGRO presence:** Absent
- **Trends:** "Scalp serum" is rising — 2026 is being called "the year of the scalp serum" by beauty editors.
- **Opportunity:** High. The category is editorially hot and no single brand dominates organic results. Product page optimization with "scalp serum" terminology is essential.

### Branded (2 keywords | 600 monthly volume)
- **ProGRO presence:** soapboxsoaps.com ranks #1 for "soapbox hair growth serum" but serves wrong product ("Oh My Growth"). "ProGRO density plus serum" shows Soapbox TikTok at #1 but the actual product page doesn't rank.
- **Opportunity:** Critical fix needed. The ProGRO product page must be optimized to rank for its own brand name. Currently, Vegamour's GRO+ product appears at #3 for "progro density plus serum" — a competitor is capturing ProGRO brand searches.

## Methodology

- **Search data:** SerpAPI Google Search API, 28 keyword queries, location: United States, top 10 results per query
- **Trends data:** SerpAPI Google Trends API, 15 keywords across 3 comparison batches, 12-month US timeseries
- **Amazon data:** Search Query Performance report export, March 2026, top 25 keywords by search volume
- **Volume estimates:** Calibrated using Google Trends relative interest proportions cross-referenced with Amazon Search Query Performance absolute volumes
- **Date of data pull:** April 1, 2026
- **Limitations:**
  - Search volume estimates are directional, not exact (Google does not provide exact volumes outside of Google Ads)
  - Google Trends measures relative interest within each comparison batch; cross-batch comparisons are approximated
  - Single-point-in-time SERP snapshot — rankings fluctuate daily
  - Free-tier SerpAPI: 31 API calls used (28 search + 3 trends) of 100/month budget
  - Only US results checked; international markets not assessed
